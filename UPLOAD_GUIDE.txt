REQUIRED GITHUB SETUP FOR AUTOMATION:
- Go to your repository on GitHub: https://github.com/arib-akhtar/arib-akhtar
- Click "Settings" > "Actions" > "General"
- Under "Workflow permissions", ensure "Read and write permissions" is selected and click Save.

HOW TO PREVIEW LOCALLY:
- Double-click `PREVIEW.html` or open it with any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) to view the complete profile styling.
